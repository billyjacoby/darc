# Darc Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

Darc is a command palette for your browser. This policy explains what data Darc reads, what it does with that data, and what it stores.

## The short version

Darc runs entirely in your browser. It has no servers, no analytics, and no tracking. It never sends your data anywhere, and it never sells or shares it.

## What Darc reads, and why

Darc reads the following only to show you results in the palette while you're searching:

- **Open tabs** (titles and addresses), so you can search and switch between them.
- **Browsing history**, so you can search pages you've visited and get address suggestions as you type.
- **Bookmarks**, so you can search and open them.
- **Most visited sites**, to rank address suggestions.
- **The page you open the palette on.** When you press the shortcut or click the toolbar button, Darc adds the palette to that page so it can appear there. It reads the page's address only if you choose **Copy Link**.

Website icons come from your browser's own icon cache, not from an outside service.

All of this stays on your device. Darc doesn't send any of it anywhere, and doesn't keep its own copy.

## What Darc stores

Darc saves only its own settings: theme, font size, and where you've dragged the palette. They're saved in your browser's extension storage. If you've turned on browser sync, your browser may sync these settings to your account, the same way it syncs other extension settings. Darc doesn't store your history, bookmarks, tabs, or searches.

## When information leaves your browser

Only when you ask for it:

- If you choose **Search for "…"**, Darc opens a Google search for your text in a new tab, just as if you had searched from the address bar.
- If you open a site, Darc opens it in a new tab like any other link.
- If you choose **Copy Link**, Darc puts the address on your clipboard.

## Permissions

Each browser permission Darc asks for is used only for the features described above. The [store listing notes](store/LISTING.md#permission-justifications) explain each one.
